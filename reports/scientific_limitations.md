# Scientific limitations

1. Passing unit/integration tests ≠ acoustic validation.
2. EWSD currently uses observed-scalar log-ratio, not validated \(F(D_1,\ldots,D_k)\).
3. Mute spectral transfer not fitted without spectra.
4. Physical covariates (β, force, velocity) not invented from dynamics labels.
5. Bibliography qualitative trends are not auto-converted to tight numerical means.
6. Harmonics / multiphonics incomplete.
7. Frequentist intervals are approximate; Bayesian HDI requires `[bayes]`.
8. Literature PDFs must be identity-validated before activating tighter priors — directory `STRINGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGGS` not auto-trusted by filename.
